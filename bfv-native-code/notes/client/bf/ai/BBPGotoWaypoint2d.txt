## file
Plan builder for ground vehicles following waypoints.

## 00990d60
buildPlan: when the bot has a vehicle and a current waypoint it compares the bot position with the waypoint (squared distance against (scalar * 4) squared, where the scalar is the vehicle's size from bfv_component_event2_scalar). When close enough it advances the waypoint (ai_00972d50). It then clears the current plan and builds a plan of BAPConPosition (radius at least 50) and a BAPALookAhead (look distance 5 times a setting) for moving to the waypoint.
