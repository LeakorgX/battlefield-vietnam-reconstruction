## file
BAPDebugger is the BotScriptDebugger used for bot action plans. It keeps two
maps of watched statements (this+4 and this+0x10) and a list at this+0x1c, and
passes a statement's debug text on at a level chosen per watch entry.

## 009dcbc0
vf2: clears the debugger's state (BAPDebugger_vf2_helper_009dcb60) and the list
at this+0x1c.

## 009dcbe0
Removes the first element of the list at this (head this+4, count this+8).

## 009dcc10
Removes the statement passed in from the list at this+0x1c (head this+0x20,
count this+0x24): returns 1 when it was found and removed, else 0.

## 009dcc90
Looks the statement up in the map passed in (std__Tree_find_009dcab0). When it
has an entry whose filter (+0x14) is -1 or matches the argument, takes the
smallest of the entry's level (+0x10), the argument level and this+0x28, gets
the statement's text through its slot 0x14 and dispatches on that level (0..3)
through the jump table at 0x9dcd58.

## 009dce30
vf1(statement, ..., kind, ...): kind 0 uses the map at this+4, kind 1 the map
at this+0x10; other kinds do nothing. Then calls the lookup above.
