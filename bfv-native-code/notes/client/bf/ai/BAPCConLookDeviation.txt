## file
Plan condition: true when the bot's view direction is close enough to the direction a component wants to look.

## 009f5140
Resolves the bot's own object. Asks the component at this+0xc (slot 0x18) for a wanted direction and a valid flag; false when not valid. When this+0x10 is set the direction is flattened (y = 0) and normalized and compared against the flattened, normalized forward row (+0x20) of the transform at bot slot 0xb0; otherwise the full 3D direction is normalized and compared with the same row. Lengths below 0.0001 (flat) or 0.001 (3D) give false. True when the dot product, the cosine of the deviation angle, is at least this+8.
