## 00a032f0
Lead solver for a gravity-free intercept. From the target's relative position and velocity (this+0x48..+0x5c) and the projectile speed it builds the coefficients of a fourth-order polynomial in time, solves it with sub_0076ed10, and keeps the non-negative root whose predicted point (Aimer_00a02c30) is closest to the target path. Writes the best time and aim point to the outputs and returns whether any root was usable.
